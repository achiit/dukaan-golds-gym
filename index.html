From the gym owner

Why this gym is run the way it is

My elder brother Sanjay got me into lifting in 2009, in a small room with two benches and one rusted rack. I still remember him standing behind me, saying “weight kam rakho, form pehle.” That one line is why I care so much about how people train here.

Boring Road has many gyms. I wanted ours to feel different: space to move, equipment that is cleaned every day, and trainers who walk over and correct you without being asked.

Come in, look at the floor, talk to the trainers, and decide for yourself.

  • Spacious floor, no waiting in a queue for the rack
  • Trainers who guide your form, not just count reps
  • Equipment cleaned daily
  • Beginner-friendly orientation on your first visit
  • Fees told upfront, before you pay anything
Form first. Weight second. Results follow.
